Bento mosaic

Asymmetric tiles of deliberately unequal size. One hero — days together — anchors the top-left, and everything else is sized by how often you look at it, not by how much data it holds. Because the grid is irregular, the eye gets a path through the page instead of scanning a wall of identical cream rectangles. Best layout for a wide desktop and for a demo: it photographs well and the colour language is visible in a single screenshot.
