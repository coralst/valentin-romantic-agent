Layout proposal 2 of 5 · profile page + sidebar variant B

Timeline spine

A relationship is a story in time, so the page is organised as one. A single coloured spine runs down the middle — gradient from rose (soon) through amber (deadlines) to grey (far off) — with cards alternating left and right and era markers breaking it into This week, This month, Standing knowledge. Facts that have no date live at the bottom in the "always true" era. Strongest narrative layout, and the most emotionally warm of the five; slightly less efficient per pixel than the bento.
