Layout proposal 3 of 5 · profile page + sidebar variant C

Category columns

Three vertical lanes, each with a solid coloured header that names the question it answers: What's coming, What to do about it, What I know about her. Inside a lane, cards inherit the lane's hue, so grouping is structural rather than implied. A five-cell gradient band under the header carries the numbers you glance at without reading. This is the most scannable and the most obviously organised of the five — the safest choice if the goal is "the page should stop feeling monotone" with the least new interaction to build.
