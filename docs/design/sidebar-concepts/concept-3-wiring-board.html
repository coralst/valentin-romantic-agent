Concept 3

Wiring board — connections as scoped permissions

The grown-up version. Same fan-out idea, but the unit of display is a capability with limits: what the agent may do, up to what amount, with what data. Clicking Connect opens a consent sheet with a spend cap — that's the slide that answers "you let an AI order things?". Tabs let the same page show the raw tool list, which is a straight line to the live-architecture story.
