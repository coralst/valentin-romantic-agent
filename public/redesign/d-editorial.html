Who she is

She goes by Sam, though only to people she'd call on a Sunday. Her love language is quality time, which is a polite way of saying she would rather have your evening than your money.

What she loves is specific and it repeats: Northern Italian food — anything with brown butter and sage — indie folk with close harmonies, and four hobbies she rotates through depending on the weather: pottery, sunrise trail runs, watercolour sketching, bread baking. Deep sage green, every time you ask. Warm and woody scents: fig, cedar, a little vanilla.

She dresses relaxed and tactile — linen, oversized knits, one good silk scarf. And somewhere in her, permanently, is Kyoto during cherry blossom season, which she mentions the way other people mention the weekend.

“She prefers to choose.”
Ask first. A surprise would land as pressure, not delight — this is the one thing I'd override a good idea for.

Her people

Four names, three of which I have straight. Ruth — Ruthie — is her mother and calls on Sundays. Nadia is the older sister, the one she tells things to first. Tom she has known since university, and he already knows every story.
