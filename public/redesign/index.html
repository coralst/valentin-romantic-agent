Focus rail

Keeps the tab bar's speed and deletes its cost: a sticky icon rail that jumps rather than filters, highlighting itself from scroll position. Nothing hidden, ⌘F works, and it tells you where you are — the one thing the old tabs never did. Paired with the C · Now + folds rail. This is the one now shipping in the React app.

 E

Her file, in place

The refactor of E. Everything with a deadline moves into the always-on rail — her face, Coming next (three events), Pinned every year (her birthday, Valentine's, Tu B'Av, the last two sharing a heart) and What to do next. That frees the board to read top to bottom in three bands: the four-week calendar beside the to-do list, Everything I know as icon-led tiles, and her family drawn as an actual four-generation tree across the whole measure. One countdown in the window, no progress meter, and it opens where the conversation renders instead of on a page of its own.
